Borrow · Weekend trading

Buying power that doesn’t stop at Friday’s close.

Your customers already trade on weekends, at venues that never close. pathfinder turns the portfolio they hold with you into weekend buying power inside your own app. Think of it as a home-equity line on their investments, open when every other lender is closed.

  • Nothing is sold.The portfolio stays put and keeps every dividend.
  • You stay lender of record.Credit runs under the permissions you already hold, with no re-KYC.
  • Regulated venues only.Buying power spends only at US-regulated 24/7 venues on a fixed list.
  • It settles itself.Monday at the open, the line repays, collateral releases and weekend P&L lands in the account.

Hold · Stablecoins

A bitcoin-backed dollar that pays, every hour of the week.

Most stablecoins pay their holders nothing. pathfinder builds and runs yield-bearing digital dollars backed by bitcoin-backed credit and US Treasury bills. Interest accrues every second, weekends included, and holders can mint or redeem at any hour.

  • Accrues every second.Business-day income becomes a per-second accrual, Saturday and Sunday included.
  • Mint and redeem 24/7.A liquidity buffer sized by our risk engine pays redemptions while markets are closed.
  • A buffer takes the first loss.A safety buffer sits beneath every holder and absorbs losses first, so the dollar is built to hold $1.
  • Approved wallets only.Minted to screened wallets, with transfer rules enforced onchain.
